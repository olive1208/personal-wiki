# Tech Hiring Process

## Summary
The roles workshop listed the usual tech hiring steps: online assessments, a recruiter phone screen, a hiring manager interview, and a take-home assignment. At the Amazon info session, the process was described as application, then online assessment, then interview. Amazon advised starting preparation now and applying early.

## Key details
- General tech hiring steps: online assessments (for example Amazon and Google), recruiter phone screen (tell me about yourself, why this role), hiring manager interview, take-home assignment (Tech roles primer).
- Recruiting is on a rolling basis (Tech roles primer).
- Before applying: target the right roles, tell a story about why you fit the role, and create a target list of companies (Tech roles primer).
- Amazon's process: application, then online assessment, then interview (Amazon info session).
- At Amazon you can apply to multiple roles but will only interview for one role (Amazon info session).
- Amazon shares information about the online assessment when you receive the interview (Amazon info session).
- Amazon interviews are behavioral and use the STAR method (Amazon info session).
- Amazon advised applying early: the earlier you apply, the better (Amazon info session).
