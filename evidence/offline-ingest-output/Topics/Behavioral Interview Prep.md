# Behavioral Interview Prep

## Summary
This page provides advice on preparing for behavioral interviews by emphasizing the use of the STAR method and structuring resume bullets effectively. It also outlines key themes for resume writing, focusing on data-driven decision-making, handling ambiguity, stakeholder coordination, and demonstrating impact.

## Key details
- The STAR method for behavioral interviews involves describing the situation, task, action (using "I" instead of "we"), and result (using data points and quantifiable results) (Behavioral Interview Prep).
- When using the STAR method, examples should focus on actions like launching initiatives, outreach, strategy, or implementation (Behavioral Interview Prep).
- It is important to tie stories to leadership principles and show the scope of impact, such as impacting executives, customers, or various stakeholders (Behavioral Interview Prep).
- Resume writing for tech roles should focus on nouns, roles (which also function as functions), and companies (Rewrite tech resume).
- Key matters for resume bullets include working with data to make decisions, operating under ambiguity, coordinating across stakeholders, scaling (people, budget, system, process), iterating, and using tools/systems (Rewrite tech resume).
- Resume bullets should focus on problems, skills, details, and the value to the business (Rewrite tech resume).
- A bullet bank is recommended for resume preparation (Rewrite tech resume).
- The process for the application and interview involves an online assessment, followed by an interview (Application -> Online assessment -> Interview (prep start now)) (Behavioral Interview Prep).
- For MBA roles, sponsorship is mostly provided (Sponsorship) (Behavioral Interview Prep).
- It is advised to do LinkedIn research and prepare the resume, applying earlier is better (Sponsorship).
