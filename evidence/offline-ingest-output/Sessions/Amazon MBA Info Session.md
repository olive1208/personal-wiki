# Amazon MBA Info Session

## Summary
Amazon offers several MBA programs including ALA, FLDP, and MLDP, each with different focuses and structures. The interview style is behavioral, emphasizing the STAR method to structure responses around situations, tasks, actions, and quantifiable results. The application process involves an online assessment and interviews, and preparation should focus on gathering examples and linking them to leadership principles.

## Key details
- Amazon emphasizes the philosophy of day 1, move fast, think big, and never settle (Amazon info session).
- The ALA program is product focused (product leadership). It launched last year and is based in Seattle. Participants own a product initiative their VP cares about, such as device strategy or AI agents. The cohort is 18 people this year and 30 people next year (Amazon info session).
- ALA internship interviews are held in person in January (Amazon info session).
- The FLDP program involves a two-year rotational experience where participants act as finance managers, pulling data, making recommendations, and diving into root causes (Amazon info session).
- The MLDP is a three-year rotational program covering roles such as senior vendor manager, product manager, and supply manager, allowing exposure to every angle of the retail business (Amazon info session).
- HRLDP was also listed as a program; the notes give no details about it (Amazon info session).
- The senior marketing manager internship can convert to full time. Interns build brand campaigns and define go-to-market plans. It is not rotational (Amazon info session).
- Interviews are behavioral based, utilizing the STAR method: situation, task, action (using "I" instead of "we"), and quantifiable results (Amazon info session).
- When using the STAR method, examples should list actions like launching XYZ, outreach, strategy, or implementation and tie them to leadership principles and the impact on stakeholders (Amazon info session).
- The application process involves an Application, an Online assessment, and Interviews (Amazon info session).
- Applicants can apply to multiple roles but will only interview for one role (Amazon info session).
- Sponsorship for MBA roles is mostly provided (Amazon info session).
- It is advised to do LinkedIn research, research, and prepare a resume, and to apply earlier (Amazon info session).
