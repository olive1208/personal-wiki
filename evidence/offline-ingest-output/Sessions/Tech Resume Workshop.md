# Tech Resume Workshop

## Summary
Effective resume bullets for tech roles should focus on demonstrating how you used data to make decisions, operated under ambiguity, coordinated with stakeholders, scaled efforts, iterated, and utilized specific tools or systems. To surface your story, one should look at job descriptions for required skills, mine personal experience, and emphasize the value delivered to the business. Good technical bullets should focus on problems, skills, details, and the value provided to the business, rather than just numbers.
