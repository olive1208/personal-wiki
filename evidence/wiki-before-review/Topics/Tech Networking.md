# Tech Networking

## Summary
The advice for tech recruiting emphasizes demonstrating existing skills and functional knowledge rather than just listing experience, focusing on showing what candidates have already done. Networking is crucial, involving building relationships rather than being purely transactional, and understanding the specific roles and company stages is important for targeting opportunities. Candidates should frame their experience with a story, emphasizing traits like a builder mentality, dealing with ambiguity, and technical fluency.

## Key details
- Candidates should show what they have already done and their relevant skills, focusing on transferable skills (Tech roles primer).
- Candidates should demonstrate functional knowledge they possess (Tech roles primer).
- Candidates should use AI to show what they have done, such as using it to create a PRD (Tech roles primer).
- Networking involves sending updates and trying to build relationships rather than making them purely transactional (Tech internship panel).
- For roles like Product Manager, the focus is on coordinating the product, cross-functional leadership, and obsession with building and user understanding (Tech roles primer).
- The hiring process includes online assessments, recruiter phone screens, hiring manager interviews, and take-home assignments (Tech roles primer).
- Candidates should ask about the product stage and company stage when networking (Networking & recruitment).
- Compelling qualities for networking include a builder mentality, ambiguity and speed, interest in product and user, technical fluency, translation of skills, and specific company and product knowledge (Networking & recruitment).
