# Tech Hiring Process

## Summary
The process of tech hiring involves understanding the role, demonstrating relevant skills and functional knowledge, telling a story about candidacy, and networking. For Amazon, the process includes online assessments, recruiter phone screens, hiring manager interviews, and take-home assignments, emphasizing a "day 1, move fast, think big" mentality.

## Key details
- Candidates should show relevant skills and functional knowledge, focusing on transferable skills (Tech roles primer).
- Candidates should tell a story explaining why they are a candidate for a role (Tech roles primer).
- The hiring process at Amazon generally involves online assessments, a recruiter phone screen, a hiring manager interview, and a take-home assignment (Amazon info session).
- For MBA programs at Amazon, programs like ALA involve owning a product initiative, serving as a strategic advisor, or rotational experience (Amazon info session).
- Interviews should use the STAR method, focusing on situation, task, action (using "I" not "we"), and quantifiable results (Amazon info session).
- Candidates should research the product stage, company stage, and focus on demonstrating a builder mentality, ambiguity and speed, and technical fluency (Networking & recruitment).
- The application process generally flows through Application, Online assessment, Interview, and further steps (Process).
