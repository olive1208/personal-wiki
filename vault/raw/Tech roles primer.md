# tech companies  - different strategies and roles

### roles

example - integrate gemini into gmail:
Product:

- product management:
  - design the why, start the nudget, align their team
  - product manager is the owner
  - domain strengthen: deep customer understanding, know users well
  - hunger to learn the technicals - what is the trade off and how does it impact the user
- design team:
  - design the how 
  - fundamental is the same but the collaboration is different
- engineering:
  - frontend and backend engineer
  - trade offs
  - a lot of negotiatin
  - solve the problem for the user and solve the business problems
- data:
  - analytics
  - figure out data pipeline
  - PM - if we launch gemini, what is the day one success metrics
  - get everyone the same page of what we need to track
- AI:
  - expect some sort of prototype
  - more fidelity in conversation
- technical program manager:
  - you have multiple products
  - TPM is technical, manage the transitions and the launch of multiple products
  GTM
- product marketing:
  - depends on B2B or B2C
  - consumer tech: you are PMM. how we are messaging the customers, the positioning, huge or small product launching
- growth team:
  - acquisition channels
  - funnel of the growth: customer journey 
  - referral programs
  - look at growth levers to get people buying the product
- sales team:
  - vertical sales team
- customer support / customer success
Horizontal: 
- Strategy + biz ops: like a internal consulting team. But in Uber / Doordash those kind of companies, ops is the execution 
- Biz dev: partnerships. for example netfilx launch on different TVs
- corp dev / finance: acquisitions M&A, financial health
- legal/HR/safety&trust

### companies

- In Google, how something is built will dominant why 
- Facebook, is also engineer-led
- Netflix, is product led. This is a human problem to get people to watch
- Apple doesn't have the PM role

### what shapes a role

- industry: domain experience
- company stage: how much ambiguity you are expected to operate in
- product focus: who is your key user
  - consumer
  - enterprise
  - internal
- product stages:
  - 0-1
  - scaling
  - mature
  - declining 
  - it decides whether you are doing the discovery or optimization
