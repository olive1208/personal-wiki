# Tech Networking

## Summary
Panelists said networking was central to landing tech internships: with alumni, second-year students, classmates, and recruiters. The advice is to build real relationships and send updates, rather than make contact purely transactional. The roles workshop added that you should know what you want to learn from each person, and ask about the product stage and company stage.

## Key details
- Network with alumni and second-year students, and ask them to introduce you to the next person (Tech internship panel).
- Look for recruiters and hiring managers (Tech internship panel).
- One panelist networked with Amazon recruiters many times when they came to campus, went to the speaker series, and networked with people at Amazon, including classmates and EWMBA students there (Tech internship panel).
- Know which conversations you need to impress in, such as with senior people at the company, and know the industry (Tech internship panel).
- Send updates and build relationships rather than making them transactional (Tech internship panel).
- Keep a networking list; the digital marketing club was also mentioned (Tech internship panel).
- One panelist met a contact during bar of the week (Tech internship panel).
- Before a conversation, decide what you want to know from this person (Tech roles primer).
- Ask about the product stage and the company stage (Tech roles primer).
- Qualities that come across as compelling: builder mentality, ambiguity and speed, interest in product and user, technical fluency, translation of your skills, and specific company and product knowledge (Tech roles primer).
