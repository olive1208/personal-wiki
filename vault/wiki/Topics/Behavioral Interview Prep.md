# Behavioral Interview Prep

## Summary
Amazon's interviews are behavioral, so the session advised preparing many stories in STAR format and tying each one to the leadership principles. The resume workshop listed what matters to tech employers (working with data, ambiguity, coordinating stakeholders, scale, iteration, tools) and suggested keeping a bullet bank.

## Key details
- The STAR method for behavioral interviews involves Situation, Task, Action (using "I" not "we," focusing on responsibility), and Result (using data points and quantifiable results) (Amazon info session).
- When preparing stories, list examples of actions such as launching initiatives, outreach, strategy, or implementation and tie them to leadership principles and the impact made on stakeholders (Amazon info session).
- It is important not to tell the same story every time and to pull different examples (Amazon info session).
- Key matters for resume bullets include working with data to make decisions, operating under ambiguity or with incomplete information, coordinating across stakeholders, scaling (people, budget, system, process), iterating, and using tools/systems (Rewrite tech resume).
- Resume bullets should focus on problems, skills, details, and value to the business (Rewrite tech resume).
- A bullet bank is recommended for resume writing (Rewrite tech resume).
- Leadership principles are very important (Amazon info session).
- Show the scope of your impact, for example on executives, customers, or large groups of stakeholders (Amazon info session).
