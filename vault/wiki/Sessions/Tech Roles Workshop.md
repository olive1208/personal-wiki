# Tech Roles Workshop

## Summary
This workshop covers how to approach MBA roles in tech by emphasizing demonstrating relevant skills and functional knowledge. It outlines various roles, the hiring process, and factors that shape a role, such as industry, company stage, and product focus. Success in this area involves telling a compelling story, demonstrating a builder mentality, and possessing technical fluency.

## Key details
- To show relevance, candidates should highlight skills they have already done and transferable skills (Tech roles primer).
- Candidates should demonstrate functional knowledge and show what they have done with specific technologies, such as using AI to create a PRD (Tech roles primer).
- Roles discussed include Product Manager, Product Marketing, Corporate Development (M&A), Strategy, Business Operations, Corporate Finance, Go-to-Market/Business Development, and Customer Support/Success (Tech roles primer).
- The hiring process involves online assessments from companies like Amazon and Google, recruiter phone screens, hiring manager interviews, and take-home assignments (Tech roles primer).
- Roles are shaped by factors such as industry, company stage, product focus (consumer, enterprise, internal), and product stages (0-1, scaling, mature, declining) (Tech roles primer).
- Networking involves asking about the product stage and company stage of a company (Tech roles primer).
- Compelling attributes for candidates include a builder mentality, ambiguity and speed, interest in product and user, technical fluency, translation of skills, and specific company and product knowledge (Tech roles primer).
