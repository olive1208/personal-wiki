# Tech Resume Workshop

## Summary
Effective tech resume bullets should focus on demonstrating how you used data for decisions, operated under ambiguity, coordinated with stakeholders, scaled efforts, iterated, and utilized specific tools and systems. To surface your story, you should look at job descriptions for required skills, mine your experience, and emphasize the value your skills brought to the business. Good tech bullets should focus on problems, skills, details, and the value to the business, rather than just numbers.
